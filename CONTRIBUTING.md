# Contributing

English · [简体中文](CONTRIBUTING.zh-CN.md) · [Website](https://weusedthis.com/)

[Add a product →](https://github.com/leontloveless/dead-ai-products/issues/new?template=product.yml)

Provide the product name and its website or another relevant link. A short description is optional. That is all you need to contribute.

You can also submit a PR to add or edit `data/products/<id>.json`:

- `name`: product name, required.
- `website`: product website or relevant link, required.
- `description`: one sentence in English, optional.

Use a lowercase, hyphen-separated filename for a new product. Keep existing filenames unchanged. The filename is the product ID; do not add an `id` field.

Maintainers handle verification, additional details, and translations in the website repository.

By contributing original data or writing, you agree to share it under [CC BY 4.0](LICENSE.md).
